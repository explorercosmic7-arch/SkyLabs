01 · Overview

What Cloud Class is

Cloud Class is a live classroom space where teachers guide lessons and students experiment, answer questions, and collaborate together in the moment.

Shared Space

One room for lessons and practice

Instead of jumping between scattered links, teachers and students stay in one active session with shared labs, checks for understanding, and live updates.

Active Learning

Hands-on instead of passive slides

Students test ideas directly, respond to live polls and quizzes, and work through problems together while the class is happening.

Clear Roles

Built for both teachers and students

Teachers lead the session and guide pacing, while students join smoothly, follow along, and ask for help when they need it.

02 · Live Connection

How teachers and students connect

Teachers open a live class session and share access so students can step straight into the room, see what is active, and stay synced as the lesson moves.

Step 01 · Host

Teachers start and run the room

A teacher opens the class session, sets the lesson flow, launches activities, and keeps everyone aligned from one control view.

Step 02 · Join

Students enter the active session

Students join the class with their name and class code or invite, landing directly in the live room without complicated setup.

Step 03 · Sync

Everyone stays in the same flow

When the teacher switches focus to a lab, quiz, or poll, students see the prompt right away and participate together in real time.

05 · Checks & Votes

Live quizzes and classroom polls

Check understanding in real time with fast questions or gather quick opinions from the whole class without slowing down the momentum.

Live Quizzes

Launch focused questions during a lesson to see how students apply what they just tested in a lab or heard in an explanation.

Instant classroom snapshot

Teachers see right away where answers line up and which ideas need another pass.

Tied to live concepts

Questions connect directly to the topic at hand rather than feeling like a separate test.

Clear next steps

Results help guide the very next explanation, lab adjustment, or discussion.

Quick Polls

Ask fast check-in or choice questions so every student can weigh in at once, even quieter learners who prefer not to speak up on mic.

Everyone gets a voice

Students respond in seconds instead of waiting for one or two hands to go up.

Gauge confidence early

Check whether the class feels ready to move forward or wants another example first.

Spark group discussion

Shared choices create natural talking points for why different students picked different options.

06 · Collaboration

Teams and co-pilots

Work together in smaller groups or pair up with a co-pilot so students can compare approaches, divide tasks, and keep moving when a challenge gets tricky.

Group Work

Student Teams

Bring classmates together around shared challenges, lab trials, or group questions during a live session.

  • Compare different answers and test ideas side by side.
  • Split roles when working through multi-step problems.
  • Keep group momentum going while the teacher checks in across the room.
Guided Support

Class Co-Pilots

Get extra guidance alongside your work to clarify concepts, stay on track, and break down the next step.

  • Ask follow-up questions without holding up the whole class.
  • Break complex prompts into smaller, easier steps.
  • Reinforce what you just practiced in labs, quizzes, and polls.
07 · Attention & Pace

Focus and class pulse

Keep attention anchored on the current activity while giving teachers a clear sense of how the room is keeping up.

Attention

Stay locked on the task

Students see the active step clearly so side distractions do not pull them away from the lesson.

Room Signal

Read the class pulse

Teachers can tell whether students are cruising through, taking their time, or hitting a wall.

Pacing

Adjust on the fly

Slow down for a tricky concept or move ahead smoothly when everyone has got it.

Clarity

Catch stuck moments early

Spot where learners need extra guidance before confusion snowballs at the end of class.
