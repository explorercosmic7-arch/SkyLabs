What is SkyLabs?

SkyLabs is an interactive space learning lab where you can explore how rockets fly, how planets and satellites stay in orbit, and how real space missions work. Instead of only reading long textbooks, you test ideas visually and learn by doing.

Whether you are a student starting with basic space science, a teacher running a live class, or someone curious about rockets and astronomy, SkyLabs brings clear simulations and guided lessons together in one simple place.
