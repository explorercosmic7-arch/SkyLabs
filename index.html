What is SkyLabs?

SkyLabs is a browser-based space lab. Run 3D rocket simulations, practice orbital missions, explore exoplanets, and use aerospace guides and calculators — all without installing software.

Change thrust, mass, and trajectory. Watch delta-v, TWR, and reentry heating respond in real time. Learn by flying the mission, not only by reading about it.
